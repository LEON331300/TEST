# Sourced media ledger

The three gallery cards use NASA media downloaded 2026-10-14 from the official [NASA-3D-Resources repository](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources). That repository states the assets are free and without copyright; see [NASA media usage guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). Their use here does not imply NASA endorsement. Each screen visibly says `VISUAL STUDY`, and these assets are illustrative rather than LEON client work.

| Local asset | Source | On-screen use |
|---|---|---|
| `nasa/moon-bootprint.webp` | [Moon – Apollo Astronaut Bootprint preview](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/blob/master/Images%20and%20Textures/Moon%20-%20Apollo%20Astronaut%20Bootprint/preview.webp) | Gallery, ~3–6s |
| `nasa/mars.webp` | [Mars preview](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/blob/master/Images%20and%20Textures/Mars/preview.webp) | Gallery, ~3–6s |
| `nasa/earth.webp` | [Earth (A) preview](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/blob/master/Images%20and%20Textures/Earth%20(A)/preview.webp) | Gallery, ~3–6s |

All interface and abstract design-card art is original procedural canvas work. The LEON logo and identity board came from the user's supplied artwork. The audio was composed procedurally for this film.
